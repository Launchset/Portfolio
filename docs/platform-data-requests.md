# Launchset: public-authority data requests

Status: **Adopted by John Helyar on 1 October 2026.** Owner: John Helyar trading as Launchset. John reviewed the proposed four rules and agreed to adopt them. This adoption authorises the corresponding procedure answers in the Meta draft; it does not authorise final submission or any disclosure of customer data.

This is an internal operating procedure for John Helyar trading as Launchset. It covers requests from police, government and other public authorities for customer data held by Launchset Connect. It does not authorise an agent to disclose data.

## Procedure

1. **Check the request.** Verify the authority and requester independently. Record the purpose, legal authority, jurisdiction, requested data and deadline. John must establish a lawful basis before any disclosure; obtain legal advice where validity, sensitive data or overseas authority is uncertain.
2. **Question invalid or excessive requests.** Seek clarification, narrowing or a legal challenge where appropriate and legally permitted. Do not disclose data solely because someone claims to represent an authority. Respect binding legal requirements and deadlines.
3. **Share only what is necessary.** John approves the exact, lawful disclosure. Exclude unrelated people and records, and use secure delivery. There is no automatic disclosure by the application or an agent.
4. **Keep a private record.** Record the request, assessment, legal advice, decision, reasons, decision-maker, exact disclosure and response date. Protect the record and supporting evidence. Never put customer data, live requests or secrets in Git or public documentation.
